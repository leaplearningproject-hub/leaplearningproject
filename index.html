Our Mission

Bridging research,
education & advocacy.

We advocate for greater investment in underfunded conditions, raise awareness through educational initiatives, and empower communities with the knowledge to recognize, understand, and advocate for their health. Rather than simply raising awareness, LEAP exists to help bridge the gap between scientific research, education, and community action through storytelling, advocacy, and meaningful engagement.

The Founder

Brittania Evelyn

Founder, LEAP

Once I learned about my family’s experience with endometriosis, I began to wonder and worry about how many other women go through this.

At the time, treatment focused on managing symptoms with birth control because there were limited options to diagnose and treat the underlying condition. Over the years, doctors conducted countless ultrasounds and tests but failed to find the answer. Unfortunately, the condition continued to worsen until it resulted in having a hysterectomy.

But my story is not a unique one. Many women live with similar conditions for years while women’s health research continues to lag behind. Despite remarkable advances in medicine, there are still diseases that lack accurate methods of diagnosis.

This realization inspired me to establish LEAP. Today, LEAP exists to help every family receive the answers, advocacy, and hope they deserve.
